About me

Hi! I’m Marie Zendee Patalinghug, a passionate UI/UX Designer, Graphic Artist, and Frontend Developer based in the Philippines. With a strong eye for detail and a love for clean design, I blend creativity with functionality to craft beautiful, user-centered digital experiences.

I thrive on turning complex ideas into intuitive, engaging designs — wireframing, prototyping in Figma, creating assets in Lightroom and Photoshop, or bringing it all to life with HTML and CSS.

Always excited to learn new tools, collaborate with creative minds, and push the boundaries of digital design. Let’s create something amazing together!
